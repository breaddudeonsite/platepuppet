Storyline

One scene per line: Scene 2 | verse | Mara walks in, looks around, points at Jo | len=8 shot=close loc=table voice=greeting. Section (intro, verse, chorus, bridge, outro) sets pacing and camera. Options are all optional: len (seconds), bars, shot (wide, med, close, low, top), loc (left, right, center, back, front, or a prop name), voice (part of a voice's name), env (part of an environment's name, or none), face (camera). Write it like directions: Mara calls out to Jo, Jo turns to look at Mara, Jo replies with a wave. Each comma (or “and Name…”) is a step, played in order. Calling, waving or pointing at someone makes them turn back to you; “looks at the camera” works too. Header lines: look: Noir, floor: wood, sky: dusk, bpm: 120, trans: fade (default transition between scenes). New per-scene options: look=wonderland (switch look from that scene on), trans=fade|dip|flash|cut (how the scene is entered), td=1.5 (transition seconds), sing=Alice (that character lip-syncs the song/vocal during the scene; join several with +). Without per-scene looks, the header look covers the whole video.

Off (default): each action plays at its natural length and the story ends when it ends. On: scenes and actions are spread out to fill the whole song.

Cue sheet: direct the actors


Write it like a script, one beat per line, in order. Each line is NAME: command. Start a line with + to make it happen at the same moment as the line before.

AI director





The offline writer is a no-key fallback. Connected AI receives the loaded song, BPM/beat grid, timeline length, and a lightweight loudness map. It should use musical phrases, accents, energy and quiet passages to shape story pacing, motion, camera cuts, lighting and effects—not just place random cuts on beats. Lyrics, harmony and instrument recognition require a model/workflow that can analyze audio itself.




AI environments
Build set works with any text model: it designs a low-poly terrain with scattered trees, rocks, buildings and so on, plus a matching 360° sky, and adds it as a walkable environment. The middle stays flat and clear for your cast. Paint backdrop asks an image model for a 360° photo-style picture (OpenAI, Gemini, Together, xAI or a custom endpoint), then mirrors it so the seam wraps cleanly. Both land in Story › Assets.


AI assistant
Chat with an assistant that sees your cast, props, timeline and available music analysis. It can coordinate motion, story, camera, lighting, effects and poses to the song. Ask in plain language, e.g. “make the chorus feel bigger and cut on phrase changes.” Everything it does can be undone with ↶.

Upload your own motion files, rigged characters, 3D models, props, textures, effects references, sound, music, and video. The library is stored in this browser on this device and survives reloads. It is not automatically synced to other devices, so keep your original files backed up.

How the AI uses your library
Each upload is analysed on arrival (rig and bone count, clips and durations, morph targets, size, image shape, audio length) and sorted into a role: character, motion, environment, backdrop, picture, voice, video, model or reference. The AI sees those facts plus your tags and notes, can search the whole library, and loads assets through the matching importer. Every load is verified, so it only reports success if something actually appeared. Duplicates (same file contents) are skipped. If the role guess is wrong, change it on the card. Add notes like “heavy two-handed sword swing, 1.2 s” for the best results.
